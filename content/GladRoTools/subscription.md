**GladRoTools Pro** is a subscription that unlocks the four premium tools: Mesh Heatmap, Performance Audit, Release Checklist, and Map Render. Every new subscriber gets a **7-day free trial**.

---

## Premium Tools

| Tool | What You Get |
|------|-------------|
| [[mesh-heatmap\|Mesh Heatmap]] | Triangle density maps with hotspot detection and Studio overlay parts. |
| [[performance-audit\|Performance Audit]] | Scored audits (0–100) against Mobile/Desktop budget presets with export. |
| [[release-checklist\|Release Checklist]] | Pre-release validation of every asset reference with improvements tracking. |
| [[map-render\|Map Render]] | Top-down orthographic PNG renders via Studio raycasting with configurable resolution. |

Premium also enables **Markdown/PDF export** for audit results and checklist reports, and **audit history with diffs** so you can track improvements over time.

---

## Free Tools Stay Free

All asset browsers (Places, Decals, Sounds, Meshes, Animations), Image Tools, Video Tools, and the Model Analyzer remain free with full functionality — no paywalls, no ads.

---

## How to Subscribe

1. Open **Settings** (gear icon in the sidebar).
2. Under **Subscription**, click **Subscribe**.
3. An App Store purchase sheet appears with pricing and the 7-day free trial offer.
4. Confirm with your Apple ID. You will not be charged until the trial ends.

---

## Managing Your Subscription

- **Check status:** Settings → Subscription shows your active subscription and expiration date.
- **Cancel:** Click **Manage Subscription** to open Apple's subscription management page, or go to **System Settings → Apple ID → Media & Purchases → Subscriptions**.
- **Refunds:** Handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## FAQ

### Can I use Premium tools without subscribing?
No. Premium tools show a subscription prompt when clicked without an active subscription.

### What happens to my audit history if my subscription expires?
Your data stays on your Mac. If you re-subscribe later, all your history, exports, and settings are preserved.

### Is there a lifetime purchase option?
Not at this time. Premium is subscription-only through the App Store.

### Do I need an internet connection for Premium?
You need an internet connection to verify the subscription with App Store receipts. Once verified, most Premium features work offline. Some checks (like duplicate content detection) require Open Cloud access to download asset bytes.
