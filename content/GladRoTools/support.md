## Response Time

I aim to respond to all inquiries within 48 hours. Please include as much detail as possible — such as your macOS version, GladRoTools version, and a description of the issue — so I can assist you efficiently.

## Bug Reports and Feature Requests

When reporting a bug, attaching diagnostic logs helps me diagnose the problem quickly. Feature requests are always welcome.

### How to Find and Attach Logs

1. Open **Settings** from the menu bar (**GladRoTools → Settings...**) or press **⌘, (Command-Comma)**.
2. Scroll to the **View Logs** section at the bottom of the Settings window.
3. Click **Open Current Log** to reveal the current session's log file in Finder, or **Open Logs Folder** to browse all saved log files.
4. Attach the relevant `.log` file to your email to [gladimdim@gmail.com](mailto:gladimdim@gmail.com).

Log files are stored locally at `~/Library/Application Support/GladRoTools/Logs/` and contain only diagnostic information about the application's operation. No personal data is included.
