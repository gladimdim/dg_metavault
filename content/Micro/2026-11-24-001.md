# SUPER DESKTOP for iPhone and iPad is on the App Store

The iOS companion is published. [SUPER DESKTOP: AI Agents](https://apps.apple.com/us/developer/dmytro-gladkyi/id578141466) puts the agents running on your Omarchy PCs on an iPhone or iPad: live terminals, prompts that run on the PC, and an alert when an agent finishes. It pairs with a QR code over your own network. There is no account.

The App Store edition has a 10-day trial and an optional one-time Pro purchase, the same shape as Google Play. Android is unchanged: the [free APK](https://github.com/gladimdim/super-desktop/releases/download/v2.0.8/SUPER-DESKTOP-2.2.0.apk) unlocks every feature, and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gladimdim.superdesktop) keeps its trial.

Pairing steps and the desktop install: [superdesktop.dmytrogladkyi.com](https://superdesktop.dmytrogladkyi.com/#phone).
